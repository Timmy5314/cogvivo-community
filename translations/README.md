Most texts were translated with help from software. **Native speakers make them better.** Spotted a sentence that sounds odd? That is the best first contribution there is.

## How to help

1. **Fix a text:** open the file for your language, click the pencil icon, change the sentence and choose *Propose changes*. That opens a pull request.
2. **Add a new language:** copy `app/en.json` and `challenges/en.json` to `<code>.json` (for example `ko.json`) and translate the texts on the right side of the colon. Keep the keys on the left and anything in `{curly braces}` exactly as it is.
3. A check runs on every pull request. It makes sure the keys and `{placeholders}` match English and that there are no links or HTML in the texts.
4. We take your translation into the app. After that it shows up here and on [cogvivo.com](https://cogvivo.com).

Look for issues labelled [`translation`](https://github.com/Timmy5314/cogvivo-community/labels/translation) or [`good first issue`](https://github.com/Timmy5314/cogvivo-community/labels/good%20first%20issue). Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) before you start.
