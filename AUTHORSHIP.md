# Authorship of Cogvivo™

Cogvivo was conceived and created by **Tim Wijnands** ([Timmy5314](https://github.com/Timmy5314)). This page proves publicly, with a date, that the complete work already existed at a given moment, without revealing its contents.

## How the proof works

1. The complete work lives in a private repository: concept, master plan, blueprint, research, database design, hundreds of challenges, 150 landmark icons, every badge and the code.
2. For every recorded version, a **proof file** is made. It lists a fingerprint (SHA-256) of every file.
3. The fingerprint of that proof file is recorded for free in the **Bitcoin blockchain** through [OpenTimestamps](https://opentimestamps.org). Nobody can change that date afterwards, not even the creator.
4. The fingerprint and the timestamp file (`.ots`) are listed below and in the [`proof/`](proof/) folder. Anyone who receives the original proof file can check on opentimestamps.org that it existed on that date.
