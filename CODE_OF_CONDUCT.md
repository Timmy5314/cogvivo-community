# Code of conduct

Cogvivo is for everyone, everywhere. Here, in issues, pull requests and Discussions, we expect the same as in the app:

- **Be kind.** Disagree with ideas, never attack people.
- **Everyone is welcome**, whatever their country, language, age, gender, religion, background or skill level.
- **No harassment**, hate, sexual content, threats or spam.
- **Keep it safe.** No personal information of others, and no challenges that are dangerous or bother other people.
- **Help newcomers.** Many people here translate or contribute for the first time.

Breaking these rules can get your comments removed or you blocked from the repository. Report a problem privately to info@cogvivo.com.

The full community guidelines of the app are at [cogvivo.com/en/info/code-of-conduct](https://cogvivo.com/en/info/code-of-conduct).
